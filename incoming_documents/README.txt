# ==============================================================================
# AMBEDKAR HERITAGE ARCHIVE - INCOMING DOCUMENTS INGESTION FOLDER
# ==============================================================================

Drop your files here for automatic ingestion, OCR, and indexing into the archive!

You can drop files directly into this folder, or organize them by subfolder:

  [1] books_and_writings/        -> Books, chapters, essays (e.g. Annihilation of Caste)
  [2] speeches/                  -> Historical speeches, Constituent Assembly debates
  [3] letters/                   -> Letters, telegrams, personal correspondence
  [4] scans_and_manuscripts/     -> Scanned pages, newspaper prints (Mooknayak, etc.), photos
  [5] audio_and_video/           -> Audio recordings, historical speeches, video footage

SUPPORTED FORMATS:
- Documents:  .pdf, .txt, .docx, .md
- Scans/Images: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .tiff, .bmp
- Audio/Video:  .mp3, .wav, .m4a, .mp4

Note: Files placed here will be validated, hashed with SHA-256 for fixity preservation,
and ingested into the digital preservation system.
