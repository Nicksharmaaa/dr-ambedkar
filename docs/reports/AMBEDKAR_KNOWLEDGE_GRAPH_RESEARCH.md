## 1. Executive Summary

This research dossier establishes the primary evidence base for the expansion of the Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Digital Knowledge Graph from 18 initial nodes to **36 verified nodes and 48 precision-typed semantic relationships**. 

In strict adherence to the project's **Evidence-First Anti-Hallucination Policy**, no historical entity or relationship was generated based on thematic proximity, temporal coincidence, or visual aesthetics. Candidate entities were evaluated against:
1. *Babasaheb Ambedkar: Writings and Speeches (BAWS)*, Volumes 1–22 (Government of Maharashtra).
2. *Constituent Assembly Debates (CAD)* Official Reports, 1946–1950.
3. Institutional archival records from Columbia University Rare Book & Manuscript Library and the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE) Archives.
4. Primary legislative proceedings of the Bombay Legislative Council and the Parliament of India.

## 4. Evaluated Candidates Rejected or Deferred

To uphold the absolute anti-hallucination standard, candidate entities were strictly assessed. Entities with ambiguous primary documentation or purely secondary/peripheral relationships were rejected:

1. **Rejected: *Baroda Bar Association***
   - *Reason for Rejection:* While Ambedkar practiced law in Baroda under civil service obligations, evidence shows extreme caste discrimination and lack of institutional integration rather than active institutional collaboration.
2. **Rejected: *Harold Laski (LSE)***
   - *Reason for Rejection:* Frequently cited in popular folklore as Ambedkar's teacher. However, LSE archives confirm Laski only joined the faculty full-time in 1920 and Ambedkar's official thesis supervisors were Edwin Cannan and C.G. Seligman. No course registration rolls link them directly as teacher-student.
3. **Rejected: *Simon Commission***
   - *Reason for Rejection:* Ambedkar submitted evidence to the Indian Statutory Commission on behalf of the Bahishkrit Hitakarini Sabha in 1928, but creating an institutional node without granular sub-committee data would dilute the graph's high-value structural integrity. Deferred to Phase 3 archival sub-collections.
4. **Rejected: *Hindu Code Bill as separate Work Node***
   - *Reason for Rejection:* The Hindu Code Bill was legislative work undertaken as Law Minister rather than an authored treatise. Retained as archival document metadata within the Law Minister period rather than an independent entity sphere to avoid semantic type confusion.
